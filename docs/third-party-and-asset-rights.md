# Third-party and asset rights — proposed publication ledger

This review records provenance and unresolved decisions. It is not a new license grant, a finding of infringement, Foundation acceptance or permission to restore excluded artwork.

The [dependency inventory](../notices/dependency-license-inventory.json) records 161 resolved Cargo graph packages, including build, development and target-specific dependencies. It is not a statement that all 161 are linked into the intended executable. The final linked/distributed subset and nested native-library notices still require review. Retained license texts are available through the [notices index](../notices/README.md).

RurixForge declares Apache-2.0. Rurix and the repository-local runtime patch retain MIT OR Apache-2.0. Six local Cargo packages need license-field/scope clarification against the root license. The declared CC0-1.0 dependency must retain its declaration; the project's program-eligibility review is still pending. Node, fonts and Microsoft runtime notices are retained for review of any later distribution, without claiming that those components are included in this source-built native artifact or authorized for project re-signing.

The [asset-rights ledger](../notices/asset-rights-ledger.json) contains ten reviewed groups. All represented media remains excluded from the source-only candidate:

- Five ai-model-musume reference-card groups lack a located explicit open-content license at the reviewed revision. Permission for originals and adaptations, or separately licensed replacements, remains to be resolved.
- The GPT dragon source expressly separates the character from the source-code license. No general reuse or adaptation grant is inferred.
- DeepSeek whalechan material has noncommercial and original-artist permission questions. Source-code licensing does not settle those artwork rights.
- OEM product photographs and software-brand artwork have unconfirmed or restricted usage conditions; attribution or public downloadability alone is insufficient.
- Generated images, video and models have production records, but input permissions and redistribution terms still need review. Generation does not automatically clear reference rights.

For any included asset, record the rights holder, applicable license or written grant, covered file hashes, allowed modifications/distribution and attribution. Alternatively, select replacement files with their own verified provenance and terms. No permission request or new permission is represented as completed by these documents.

Public references in the ledger are evidence pointers, not rights grants. The ledger deliberately omits private correspondence, proposed contact messages, local application paths and applicant identity information. The remaining decision is the actual publicly distributable asset set; no asset license is invented to fill that gap.
